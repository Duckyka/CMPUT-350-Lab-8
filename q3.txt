1. The best component from Sprite to move out would be VertexArray. The reason for this is because VertexArray essentially is a bounding box (used to contain 
the sprite from the texture in this case), however bounding boxes can be used for any gameObject, specifically those that collide, or are drawn to the world. 
From here these boxes can be made specific to the object they are used for. 

2. When rendering transparent sprites onto one another, the destination sprite / colour has to be rendered first as this is the colour of the pixel the source 
will be drawn onto. With this rendered, the source can come in to be rendered on top, taking the destination colour values and "blending" with them properly.
